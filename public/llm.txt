# Nikah.Net (നിഖാഹ്.നെറ്റ്)
> Kerala's premier Muslim matrimonial advertising and proposal sharing portal focusing on Malappuram, Kozhikode, and worldwide Malayali GCC communities.

## Overview
Nikah.Net is a modern matrimonial advertising platform designed to connect genuine Muslim families seeking marriage alliances with dignity, Islamic modesty, and privacy. Unlike traditional brokers or closed marriage bureaus, Nikah.Net functions as an open matrimonial classifieds portal enabling families to publish verified matrimonial advertisements and connect directly with prospective partners.
